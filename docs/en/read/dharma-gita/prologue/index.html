Prologue

People say monks found this text on their way home to China, after a pilgrimage to India.

On the road back, they stopped for the night in a remote cave. There they found old scrolls, worn by time.

The scrolls held the teachings of a Great Master. His name was lost long ago, but his words still carry the fire of truth.

They called this text "The Song of the Way."

It followed the great book "The Art of War." But it did not speak about strategy in the outer world. It spoke about the hidden battle every person fights inside.
