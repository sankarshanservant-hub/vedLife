Preservation Through Transformation

Veda + Life: ancient knowledge, lived now.

ved·Life is a press and a cultural ecosystem around the Vedic tradition. We translate a vast body of knowledge into books, sound and experiences for contemporary life — with the rigour of a modern studio and the depth of an ancient source.

01

Let the source speak

We frame and translate; we don’t sermonise. The source, the century, the translator — specifics instead of atmosphere.

02

No costume

We honour the depth of the tradition and remove what stands between a person and the knowledge: mystification, esoteric clutter, gatekeeping.

03

The record is open

Knowledge stays alive by finding new forms. Every book is the next line in a record, and the next line is always left open.

The mark is a single square turned through every stage of its own becoming, resolving to a living centre. Preservation through transformation.
