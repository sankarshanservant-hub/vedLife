№ 001 · Dharma Gita · Prologue

Prologue

People say monks found this text on their way home to China, after a pilgrimage to India1.

On the road back, they stopped for the night in a remote cave. There they found old scrolls, worn by time.

The scrolls held the teachings of a Great Master. His name was lost long ago, but his words still carry the fire of truth.

They called this text "The Song of the Way2."

It followed the great book "The Art of War3." But it did not speak about strategy in the outer world. It spoke about the hidden battle every person fights inside.

Sources and notes

  1. India is where the monks are returning from. Sample note: in the edition this holds a source reference and a map. ↩
  2. The title is the press’s translation. Sample note: the date and the editor of the translation are given in the colophon. ↩
  3. “The Art of War” is Sun Tzu’s treatise. Here it is a reference, not a quotation. Sample note. ↩
