How it holds together

A curator

Each territory has its own curator. They open briefs, lead the cohort and answer for quality.

A source council

Every work is checked against the text: the verse is named, the translation signed. A retelling never stands in for a source.

Rights and shares

Rights to a work stay with its author. The press takes a licence for the form. Share and term are in the contract. This is a proposal, not terms.
